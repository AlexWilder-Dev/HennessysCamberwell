On the walls

Some stories are too beautiful to stay hidden in a biscuit tin.

These postcards belonged to an old lady in Connemara, kept for decades in an old tin box: little pieces of Ireland, full of memories, handwriting, journeys and homesickness. Now they’ve made their way across the sea to a new home on our walls, to be admired over a pint, good music and great company.

Every lyric on this wall means something. The songs we grew up with, the ones we sing after a few pints, and the tunes that bring everyone together.

What song would you add to our wall?

    The corner since 1839

    Four names, one corner.

    There’s been a pub at 225 Camberwell New Road since around 1839. The name over the door has changed a few times since.

    1. c.1839

      Clarence Arms

      The first name over the door.

    2. By 1856

      Clarendon Arms

      Renamed by the middle of the century.

    3. Later

      Miliki Spot

      A spell under a new name.

    4. Then

      Clarendon Arms

      The old name went back up.

    5. 2026

      Hennessy’s

      Taken over and renamed. A single bar, recently refurbished.
